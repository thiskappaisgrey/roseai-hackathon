Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the things most guests ask us.

What time is check‑out, and can it be extended?

Standard check‑out is 12:00 PM. As an anniversary guest, we're happy to extend to 2:00 PM at no charge. Later checkouts can be arranged through your concierge based on availability.

Is there a dress code at Madera?

Smart casual. Collared shirts preferred for gentlemen at dinner; no athletic wear. The bar is more relaxed.

How do I reserve a private cabana?

Cabanas are available for half‑day or full‑day reservation. Tap "Reserve" on the Picked for You card, or text your concierge — we'll handle the rest, including a chilled welcome bottle.

Can the concierge arrange a wine country day trip?

Yes — Napa and Sonoma are about 90 minutes by car. We can arrange a private driver and curated tasting itinerary tailored to your preferences. 48 hours' notice recommended.

Are bikes complimentary?

Our Blue Jay loaner bikes are complimentary for all overnight guests. Helmets, locks, and route maps included. Guided rides with our Cycling Concierge are bookable separately.

How do I access the spa and pool?

As an overnight guest, both are complimentary throughout your stay. The Asaya Spa lobby is open 7A–9P. Treatments require advance booking — we recommend reserving as soon as possible during peak season.

Is parking included?

Yes — complimentary valet parking is included with your stay. Self‑parking is also available around the property.
