Explore Menlo Park

Venture beyond the resort — the Peninsula's best dining, wine, nature, and culture are just minutes away.

🍷

Wine Country Day Trip

Napa and Sonoma are under 90 minutes north. Closer to home, visit Portola Vineyards, Thomas Fogarty Winery, or Ridge Vineyards in the Santa Cruz Mountains — all within 30 minutes.

🍴

Local Dining

Walk to Lutticken's for inventive small plates just 0.2 mi away. Downtown Menlo Park and Palo Alto offer dozens of acclaimed restaurants, from sushi to farm-to-table California cuisine.

🌳

Stanford & Nature

Stroll the Stanford University campus and its Rodin sculpture garden. For active mornings, hike The Dish trail or bike along the Bayfront Trail at Bedwell Bayfront Park.

🎨

Arts & Culture

Discover the historic Allied Arts Guild gardens (since 1929), the Computer History Museum, or browse the boutiques and galleries on Santa Cruz Avenue.

🚗

Complimentary House Car

Rosewood Sand Hill offers a complimentary house car for local excursions — perfect for reaching downtown Menlo Park, Palo Alto, or nearby attractions without the hassle.

🌊

Half Moon Bay

Just 30 minutes west, the dramatic Pacific coastline of Half Moon Bay offers cliffside walks, fresh seafood, and some of California's most romantic sunset views.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are pool and cabana hours?

The outdoor heated pool and hot tub are available daily for hotel guests. Private cabanas can be reserved through the concierge and include personalized food and beverage service.

How do I book a couples treatment at Asaya Spa?

Contact the spa directly at the front desk or through in-room dining to arrange a couples treatment. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, especially on weekends.

Does Madera accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Madera's culinary team is happy to accommodate allergies, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs. Please inform the team when making your reservation.

Can I arrange an anniversary surprise?

Of course. Our concierge team specializes in personalized celebrations — from in-room floral arrangements and champagne to private sunset dinners on the garden terrace. Just let us know.

How do I use the complimentary house car?

The house car is available for local trips within a 5-mile radius on a first-come, first-served basis. Simply contact the front desk or bell services to arrange a ride.

Is there a fitness center?

Yes. Rosewood Sand Hill features a fully equipped fitness center plus the Asaya Movement Studio offering yoga, Pilates, and guided sessions. Ask about the current schedule at check-in.

What is the cancellation policy for experiences?

Most on-property experiences and spa treatments can be cancelled up to 24 hours in advance. Off-property arrangements (wine tours, etc.) may vary — the concierge will confirm terms at booking.
